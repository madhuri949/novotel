Help & Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Find instant answers to common questions regarding check-in times, family amenities, airport transfers, and cancellation flexibility.

Standard check-in begins at 3:00 PM, and check-out is until 12:00 PM (noon). Early check-in and late check-out (up to 3:00 PM or 5:00 PM) are available upon request, subject to availability. ALL Accor Live Limitless Gold and Platinum members enjoy complimentary late check-out privileges.
At Novotel, children are our special guests. Up to two children under the age of 16 stay free of charge in their parents' room using existing bedding. Furthermore, children under 16 enjoy complimentary breakfast buffet when accompanied by paying adults having breakfast.
Novotel Grand Horizon is conveniently situated only 12 kilometers (approximately 15 minutes by private taxi or hotel chauffeur) from Nice Côte d'Azur Airport. We provide private Mercedes sedan airport transfers and helicopter shuttle transfers upon request.
Our "Flexible Hospitality Rate" permits cancellation or modification with no penalty up to 24 hours prior to your scheduled arrival (by 3:00 PM hotel local time). Non-refundable promotional rates or special holiday packages have specific terms specified at the time of reservation.
Yes, well-behaved small to medium pets (up to 12 kg) are warmly welcomed in designated pet-friendly guest rooms. We provide a plush pet bed, organic treats, and water bowls upon check-in for a nominal nightly cleaning fee.
